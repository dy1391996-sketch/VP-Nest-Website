Our story

From facility operations to a trusted stay experience.

VP Nest – The Studio99Stay was founded by Deepak Yadav, an operations and facility-management professional with hands-on experience in housekeeping, team supervision, vendor coordination and customer service.

The brand brings that operating discipline into short-stay hospitality at Gaur City Center, Greater Noida West—coordinating bookings, room readiness, guest support, housekeeping and property upkeep.
