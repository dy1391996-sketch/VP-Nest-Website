Frequently asked questions

Important answers before you confirm your stay.

For any room-specific detail, rely on the written confirmation shared by the VP Nest booking team.

How do I check availability?

Share your preferred date, check-in time, duration and number of guests on WhatsApp. We will confirm the current available option and final price.

What stay durations are available?

Depending on inventory, options may include short stays, day stays, 24-hour stays and multiple nights. Availability varies by date and room.

Do I need valid ID at check-in?

Yes. Every guest must carry a valid government-issued photo ID and comply with the confirmed property and check-in requirements.

When should I make payment?

Pay only after receiving written confirmation of the studio, date, duration, price, payment requirement and booking terms from VP Nest.

Can I select a particular room, floor or view?

Share your preference before booking. The exact studio, floor, balcony, view and amenities are subject to current availability and must be confirmed in writing.

What is the cancellation or rescheduling policy?

The applicable cancellation or rescheduling terms are shared with the booking confirmation. They may depend on notice time, date and room commitment.

Are unmarried couples allowed?

Stay acceptance is subject to valid government ID, adult age, applicable law, building rules and the conditions confirmed by the booking team.

How can I get help during my stay?

Use the confirmed support number or WhatsApp +91 9211701998. Share the issue clearly so the team can provide the next action or timeline.

Still have a booking question?

Send your requirement directly to the VP Nest team on WhatsApp.
